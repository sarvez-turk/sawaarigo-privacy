1. About this policy

SawaariGo ("we", "our", "the app") is a ride-booking app that connects customers with local drivers for bike, car and mini wheeler (auto) rides. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and what choices you have. By using the app you agree to this policy.

2. Information we collect

  • Account details: your email address and password (the password is handled by Firebase Authentication; we cannot see it), your name, and your role (customer or driver).
  • Location: if you tap "use my location", we read your device's GPS location to fill in the pickup place. If you are a driver and switch to Online, the app saves your location so that nearby ride requests can be sent to you.
  • Ride details: pickup and destination, vehicle type, distance, fare, ride status, ride time, a one-time ride OTP, and the rating a customer gives a driver.
  • Driver details: online/offline status, approval status, and the driver's name and email shown to the customer after a ride is accepted. The customer's email is shown to the driver on the ride card.
  • Push notification token: drivers' devices register a notification token so we can send new-ride alerts.

We do not collect card, UPI or bank details. Rides are paid in cash directly to the driver.

3. How we use your information

  • To create your account and let you log in.
  • To calculate distance and fare, and to match a ride request with nearby online drivers.
  • To send ride alerts and show ride status, history and ratings.
  • To keep the app secure and prevent misuse (for example, the OTP check before a driver starts a ride).

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Who can see your information

  • A driver who sees or accepts your ride can see your pickup, destination, fare and the email linked to your account.
  • A customer can see the name, email and a short ID of the driver who accepted their ride.
  • Other users cannot see your rides.

5. Services we use

To run the app we rely on these third-party services, which process data as described in their own policies:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Realtime Database, Cloud Functions) for accounts, ride data and server logic.
  • Expo and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging for push notifications.
  • LocationIQ for place search, distance and route calculation. Pickup and destination text, and coordinates, are sent to it.
  • Google Maps opens when a driver taps the navigation button.

6. How long we keep data

We keep your account and ride history for as long as your account is active, so that you can see your history and drivers can see their earnings and ratings. When your account is deleted, we delete or anonymise your personal data, except where we must keep a record for legal or safety reasons.

7. Your choices and account deletion

  • You can deny location permission and type your pickup place manually.
  • Drivers can switch Offline at any time to stop location saving and ride alerts. Logging out also sets the driver Offline.
  • You can ask us to delete your account and data by emailing drsarvez@gmail.com from your registered email address, with the subject "Delete my SawaariGo account". We will act on the request within 30 days.

8. Security

Data is stored with Firebase and protected by access rules so that users can only read the rides they are part of. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children

SawaariGo is meant for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 18.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. Please check it from time to time.

11. Contact

For questions or requests about your data, email drsarvez@gmail.com.

2. हम कौन-सी जानकारी लेते हैं

  • खाते की जानकारी: आपका ईमेल पता और पासवर्ड (पासवर्ड Firebase Authentication संभालता है, हम उसे देख नहीं सकते), आपका नाम, और आपकी भूमिका (ग्राहक या ड्राइवर)।

  • राइड की जानकारी: पिकअप और ड्रॉप की जगह, वाहन का प्रकार, दूरी, किराया, राइड की स्थिति, राइड का समय, एक बार वाला राइड OTP, और ग्राहक द्वारा ड्राइवर को दी गई रेटिंग।
  • ड्राइवर की जानकारी: Online/Offline स्थिति, अप्रूवल स्थिति, और राइड स्वीकार होने के बाद ग्राहक को दिखने वाला ड्राइवर का नाम और ईमेल। ड्राइवर को राइड कार्ड पर ग्राहक का ईमेल दिखता है।
  • पुश नोटिफिकेशन टोकन: ड्राइवर के फ़ोन का नोटिफिकेशन टोकन सेव होता है ताकि नई राइड का अलार्म भेजा जा सके।

हम कार्ड, UPI या बैंक की जानकारी नहीं लेते। किराया सीधे ड्राइवर को नकद में दिया जाता है।

3. हम जानकारी का उपयोग कैसे करते हैं

  • आपका खाता बनाने और लॉगिन कराने के लिए।
  • दूरी और किराया निकालने, और राइड को पास के Online ड्राइवरों से मिलाने के लिए।

हम आपकी निजी जानकारी बेचते नहीं हैं और विज्ञापन के लिए इस्तेमाल नहीं करते।

  • जो ड्राइवर आपकी राइड देखता या स्वीकार करता है, वह आपका पिकअप, ड्रॉप, किराया और खाते से जुड़ा ईमेल देख सकता है।

ऐप चलाने के लिए हम इन तीसरे पक्ष की सेवाओं का उपयोग करते हैं, जो अपनी-अपनी नीतियों के अनुसार डेटा संभालती हैं:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Realtime Database, Cloud Functions): खाते, राइड डेटा और सर्वर के काम के लिए।
